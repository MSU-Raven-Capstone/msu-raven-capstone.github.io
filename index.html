Project Problem Statement

  • As cities grow, so does the requirement for urban air mobility vehicles.
  • Electric vertical take off and landing vehicles (eVTOLs) are becoming more prominent, with several existing models used for development.
  • Before actual use, it is important to understand how these vehicles operate in order to maximize safety and efficiency while minimizing noise.
  • The NASA Research Aircraft for eVTOL Enabling techNologies (RAVEN) exists for use in research and development.
  • There is seemingly little aerodynamic data on this vehicle or similar platforms.
