More About the Project

Project objectives, advisors, timeline, and where to go for the full details.

Proposed Objectives

  • Further investigate existing research on the aerodynamics of the RAVEN and other tilt-rotor VTOLs.
  • Validate existing data.
  • Identify the gap in current literature.
  • Fill that gap through CFD and wind tunnel testing.
    • Possible areas to address:
    • Rotor–rotor interactions
    • Wing–rotor interactions
    • Aerodynamic acoustics
    • Aerodynamic characteristics throughout transition phases
