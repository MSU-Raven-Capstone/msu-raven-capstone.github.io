Research Approach

How the aerodynamics of the RAVEN will be investigated.

This page is still in progress. It will be updated as research methods are defined and the research is performed.

Overview

The first phase of research is a literature review to evaluate existing research and identify a gap in said research. After the literature review, experiments will be performed to obtain aerodynamics data. CFD may also be used.

Wind Tunnel Testing

The main research method will be through utilizing MSU's wind tunnel.

Research Areas

Candidate areas identified in the project proposal to address through wind tunnel testing:

  • Rotor–rotor interactions
  • Wing–rotor interactions
  • Aerodynamic acoustics
  • Aerodynamic characteristics throughout transition phases (hover → cruise)
